A farm that grows with nature, not against it

Mounam Natural Farm is a working natural farm near Coimbatore. We grow food without chemical fertilizers or pesticides, and we open the gates so you can see how it is done.

Walk the fields, pick what is in season, and learn the methods behind healthy soil. Come for a day, or come to learn something you can use at home.

What you can do at the farm

Pick one activity or spend the whole day. Everything is hands-on and guided.

Harvest fresh produce

Pick vegetables and fruits straight from the plant and take them home.

Learn natural farming

Understand composting, mulching and soil care from the farmers who use them.

Walk the food forest

Stroll through layers of trees, shrubs and groundcover that feed themselves.
